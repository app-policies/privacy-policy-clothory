# Clothory Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 1, 2026

Clothory is a simple digital closet and outfit planner. This Privacy Policy explains how Clothory handles information in version 2.0.0.

## Summary

- Clothory does not require an account.
- Clothory does not provide cloud sync in version 2.0.0.
- Clothory does not use AI features in version 2.0.0.
- Clothory does not include subscriptions or in-app purchases in version 2.0.0.
- Clothing photos you choose from your library or take with the camera, and wardrobe data, are stored locally on your device.
- The developer does not upload clothing photos or wardrobe data to a developer server.
- Clothory uses Google AdMob banner ads.
- Contact Support email is optional.

## Information You Provide

Clothory lets you add clothing photos by choosing images from your photo library or taking photos with your camera. You can also add item details, favorite status, outfits, worn logs, and notes. This information is used to provide the app's wardrobe, outfit planning, search, filtering, favorites, wear counts, and worn log features.

## Clothing Photos and Wardrobe Data

Clothing photos and wardrobe data are stored locally on your device. Camera and photo library access are used only to add clothing item images. The developer does not upload your clothing photos, wardrobe items, outfits, worn logs, or notes to a developer server.

If you delete the app, locally stored app data may be deleted from your device according to iOS behavior.

## AI

Clothory does not use AI features in version 2.0.0. The app does not perform clothing recognition, background removal, or automated outfit suggestions.

## Accounts and Cloud Sync

Clothory does not require login or account creation. Clothory does not provide cloud sync in version 2.0.0.

## Purchases

Clothory does not include subscriptions or in-app purchases in version 2.0.0.

## Advertising

Clothory uses Google AdMob banner ads. Google and AdMob may process information such as approximate location, device identifiers, advertising data, usage data, and diagnostics for ad delivery, ad measurement, fraud prevention, and related advertising purposes. Where required, Clothory requests advertising consent before loading ads and provides access to applicable advertising privacy choices from Settings.

For more information about how Google handles advertising data, review Google's privacy policies and advertising controls.

## Contact Support

Contact Support is optional. If you choose to email support, your email address and the contents of your message will be handled by your email provider and received by Clothory support for the purpose of responding to your request.

Contact: support@passmate.jp

## Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Clothory changes. The latest version will be made available through the app or the published privacy policy page.
